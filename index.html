Our Legacy

About Sabian Secondary School

Established in 1995, Sabian Secondary School has been dedicated to cultivating excellence, integrity, and lifelong learning in every student.

School History & Vision

For over two decades, Sabian has grown into a leading institution, recognized for its commitment to academic rigour and comprehensive student development. Our vision is to empower the next generation of global citizens who are equipped to face complex challenges with courage and creativity.

We continuously update our teaching methodologies and infrastructure to ensure our students receive a world-class education that respects traditional values while embracing technological innovation.

Core Values

  • Integrity: Fostering honesty and strong moral principles.
  • Excellence: Striving for the highest standard in all academic and extracurricular pursuits.
  • Respect: Promoting a culture of courtesy, tolerance, and inclusion.
  • Community: Encouraging active participation and social responsibility.
